7Method & limitations

Design decisions that protect the numbers

  • Patient-level split — the same person never appears on both sides of a split (asserted at runtime, 0 overlaps).
  • Death & hospice discharges excluded — they cannot be readmitted; keeping them would leak the answer.
  • One fitted preprocessor — training and any consumer transform with the identical object.
  • Threshold tuned on validation, test evaluated exactly once.
  • SHAP additivity asserted — the explanation is verified, not assumed.

Limitations to state honestly

  • AUC ≈ 0.68 is moderate — good for ranking a follow-up list, nowhere near autonomous decisions.
  • Data is from 1999–2008 US hospitals; external validation on a recent cohort is required.
  • 49% missing medical_specialty and 40% missing payer_code were dropped, not modelled.
  • Rank-4 importance (discharge_disposition_id_25 = “Not Mapped”) is a database artifact, not clinical signal.
  • Benchmark exercise — no calibration, fairness audit or prospective validation.
